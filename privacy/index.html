Uncomfy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Uncomfy keeps your information on your iPhone. We don't have accounts, servers, ads or analytics, so we don't collect, see, sell or share your personal data.

This policy explains how the Uncomfy iPhone app ("Uncomfy," "the app," "we," "us") handles information. Uncomfy is operated by Christian Bezzant in South Carolina, USA.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Uncomfy is stored only on your device, including:

  • Your answers from setup (name, areas, discomforts, difficulty, wake-up times, physical limits).
  • Your dares, streaks, points, progress and Comfy's Closet.
  • Proof of your dares: photos you take, optional move clips, and stick-figure replays.
  • An optional profile photo you choose.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, or use Profile → Delete my data, it's gone. Your iPhone's own iCloud or device backup may include app data, under Apple's privacy terms.

Camera, photos and motion

  • Camera: used to check your dares. Body tracking (counting reps or timing holds) and photo checks (counting handwritten lines or recognizing an object) run on your phone. Video frames are analyzed live and are not saved unless you turn on "Save a clip." Clips never include sound.
  • Photo library: only if you choose a profile photo. You pick the photo; the app can't see the rest of your library.
  • Motion sensors: used by the face-up timer to tell if your phone is lying still. Nothing is recorded.
  • "Private" dares: for some personal writing dares, the app counts your words and then discards the photo.

Screen Time and app locking

Uncomfy uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock the apps you choose and to show your screen time. Apple keeps this information private: the app only sees anonymous tokens for the apps you pick, and usage details are shown on your phone by Apple's system. Uncomfy never receives a list of your apps or your browsing.

Notifications

Reminders are created and scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in Profile or in your iPhone's Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Email

If you email us (for example, using Send feedback or Report a dare), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply and improve Uncomfy. We don't add you to a mailing list.

Children

Uncomfy is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If we ever begin collecting data, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy label first.

Contact

Questions? Email support@uncomfyapp.com.
